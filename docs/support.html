    Institutional Partnerships

    Keep the compute and the record independent.

    High-resolution empirical analysis runs on dedicated infrastructure: local compute clusters, open archival datasets, and researcher time. We collaborate with foundations, research bodies, and academic sponsors.

    Foundation & Academic Grants

    The firm collaborates closely with the Observable Compute Foundation, an independent 501(c)(3) research organization dedicated to public observability and empirical evaluation in computational entities. We accept institutional grants, compute allocation sponsorships, and formal research partnerships.

    We do not accept micro-donations or sponsorship that seeks to influence audit findings. Engagements are governed by written contracts and conflict-of-interest screening.

    Sovereign Compute Infrastructure
