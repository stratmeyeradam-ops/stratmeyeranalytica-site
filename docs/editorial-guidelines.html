Auditing & Review Guidelines

BLUF: Operational review standards and auditing parameters.

Skepticism and Compliance Rigor

Our auditing processes conform to strict regulatory frameworks (including HHS IRB guidance, HIPAA Security rules, and FERPA privacy parameters). We prioritize the following operational principles:

  • Empirical Verifiability: Every audit point must be backed by documented institutional evidence, not folk psychology or organizational habit.
  • Conflict of Interest Mitigation: Complete separation of audit oversight from operational developers.
  • Procedural Integrity: Strict step-by-step validation of ethics checklists and staff responses.
