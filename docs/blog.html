Operational Scope

Stratmeyer Analytica provides specialized consulting and auditing services for institutions and foundations. We do not provide "solutions" in the corporate sense; we identify systemic bottlenecks, flag institutional mendacity, and enforce regulatory compliance.

System Architecture & Automation

Deployment of localized, high-redundancy processing systems. Design, implementation, and scaling of automated workflows to reduce reliance on legacy manual labor and eliminate institutional bloat.

Regulatory Compliance & Auditing

Rigorous review of IRB protocols, research programs, HIPAA, and FERPA structures. We audit compliance for procedural integrity, ethical alignment, and regulatory exposure.

Behavioral Strategy

System-wide behavioral mitigation for high-acuity residential environments (PRTF/ARU). Strategic consulting on reducing physical holds, enhancing safety protocols, and optimizing staff response models.

Consulting Philosophy

Core Metric Efficiency is the only objective metric. Institutional bloat is a liability.

Our engagements are structured for specific, measurable outcomes. We review operations with structural skepticism, aiming to replace broken, high-overhead manual processes with localized, high-redundancy systems.
