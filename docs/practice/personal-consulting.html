    Personal Consulting & Executive Counsel

    High-stakes advisory for founders, research directors, and institutional leaders navigating legal-technical disputes and systemic crises.

    Confidential 1-on-1 counsel at the frontier of law and systems

    High-stakes leadership decisions cannot be delegated to generic consulting committees or junior analysts. Stratmeyer Analytica provides direct personal consulting delivered personally by Director Adam Ian Stratmeyer, J.D.

    Engagements are structured for executives, research directors, institutional founders, and board members who face high-acuity crossroads: regulatory investigations, high-risk technical deployment decisions, intellectual property boundaries in algorithmic systems, and behavioral crisis management.

    Scope of Personal Advisory

    • Strategic Executive Advisory: Confidential sounding board for technology directors navigating institutional skepticism or sudden regulatory exposure.
